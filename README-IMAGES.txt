RARSGRAM LTD website — required images
=====================================
Place these files inside:  assets/img/

Required image filenames (rename your photos to match exactly):

  logo.jpg
  sat-proto-bw.jpg
  prototypes-kit.jpg
  prototypes-row.jpg
  devices-lineup.jpg
  devices-wall.jpg
  mission-control-screen.jpg
  telemetry-archive.jpg
  rsat-login.jpg

Tips:
- Keep filenames exactly as listed (lowercase, hyphens).
- Recommended: JPG or WebP, max ~1200–1600 px wide for web.
- After renaming, upload them into assets/img/ on your host (or keep the folder structure when uploading the whole site).

The site is a single-file SPA (index.html). Open index.html in a browser or upload the whole rarsgram-site folder to any static host.
